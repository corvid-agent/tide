The keeper matures the tide; the beneficiary pulls. Each keeper tick() matures one drip into the claimable pool; claim() pays matured × drip out of the app with an inner payment (outer call must pool the fee: flat fee ≥ 2000 µALGO). Once deployed, state is read from the app's global state on the TestNet indexer; the chain is the source of truth. Contract: smart_contracts/tide/contract.py.
